CORE PRODUCT MODEL — MUST FOLLOW
============================================================

A Service is a passenger-facing transport service that operates on exactly ONE existing Route.

The relationship is:

Organization
    ↓
Route
    ↓
Service
    ↓
Service-specific Fare Configuration

IMPORTANT:

- Route does NOT own fares.
- Service owns/uses its own fare configuration.
- Different Services may use the same Route and still have different fare configurations.
- A Service must select exactly one existing Route.
- Fare must be configured for the Service.
- Passenger fare calculation later uses:
    service_id + origin_stop_id + destination_stop_id
  and the backend derives the route and that Service's fare configuration.
- The frontend must NEVER calculate the final passenger fare.

Do not revert to route-level fare configuration.
Do not attach a fare configuration to Route.
